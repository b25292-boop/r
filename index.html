Working Principle

Water from the nozzle strikes the turbine blades and produces mechanical torque. The turbine rotates a shaft connected to a magnetic rotor. As the magnetic rotor rotates inside the stationary copper winding, the changing magnetic flux induces an alternating electromotive force. The electrical load draws current and produces an opposing electromagnetic torque.
